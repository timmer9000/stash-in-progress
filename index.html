Where is all your info going?

Ten apps, ten providers, ten privacy policies. Or one place, with you holding the key.

Privacy

We can't open it.

Lock any item with end-to-end encryption. There is no way in, not for us, not for AI. Forget your passphrase and it is gone. That is the only way to mean it.

Ownership

It's yours.

Export everything, any time: Google OKF, Markdown, HTML, CSV, iCal, vCard. Nothing is held hostage.

Control

You pick what AI sees.

AI reads your notes only when you ask, and never the locked ones. Data sent to AI is anonymized, so chats are not tied to you.

Independence

Standards, not lock-in.

Open formats in, open formats out. Connect any AI through MCP. Build on the Mindshare API.

Reliability

Offline first.

Stored on your device, synced to Google Firebase, the largest BaaS (Backend as a Service) provider. Works on a plane, in a basement, in a dead zone.**AI features do not work offline.

Runs on Google CloudAI by GoogleVoice by OpenAISign-in by Google AuthLock and share: wait, how? Only with people who have keys
